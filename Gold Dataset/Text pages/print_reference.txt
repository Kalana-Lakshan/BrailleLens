
Once upon a time
there was a big jungle.
The trees were full of fruits and beautiful flowers.
All the animals and birds lived there happily.
And the king was a lion.
.....................................................


Wesak season came to the jungle.
"What are we going to do this time?"
asked lion the king.
"We will have a Wesak lantern competition"
everybody shouted.
.....................................................


"All right. The one who makes the biggest
and the most durable lantern
that doesn't break even in the rain
will be the winner", said the lion.
.....................................................


Everybody rushed to make
the winning lantern.
The baby rabbit too, though small,
wanted to make a lantern.
.....................................................


Baby rabbit went to elephant and asked,
"Could you please give me some tissue papers?"
Elephant looked at the baby rabbit and said,
"No, no. I am going to make my lantern
as big as myself.
So I need all the tissue papers I have".
Sadly left the baby rabbit.
.....................................................


Then baby rabbit went to giraffe and asked,
"Could you please give me some bamboo sticks?"
Giraffe looked at the baby rabbit and said,
"No, no. I am going to make my lantern
as tall as myself.
S I need all the bamboo sticks I have".
sadly left the baby rabbit.
.....................................................


Then baby rabbit went to snake and asked,
"Could you please give me some string?"
Snake looked at the baby rabbit and said,
"No, no. I am going to make my lantern
as long as myself.
So I need all the string I have".
Sadly left the baby rabbit.
.....................................................


Next baby rabbit went to deer and asked,
"Could you please give me some glue?"
Deer looked at the baby rabbit and said,
"No, no. I am going to make my lantern
as dotted as myself.
So I need all the glue I have".
Sadly left the baby rabbit.
.....................................................


Baby rabbit felt so lonely and helpless.
He sat under a bush and started to cry.
Lightbug, who was sleeping under a leaf
heard the crying.
.....................................................


"Why are you crying baby rabbit?"
asked lightbug.
"Nobody helps me to make a Wesak lantern",
cried baby rabbit.
"Don't worry my friend, I will help you",
saying this lightbug flew away.
.....................................................


Soon it was the Wesak night.
The jungle was brightly lit with
colourful Wesak lanterns.
.....................................................


Elephant's lantern was as big as the elephant.
Giraffe's lantern touched the tree tops.
Deer's lantern has dots all over it.
Hedgehog's lantern was full of spikes.
Crocodile's lantern was wide open as his mouth.
All the lanterns were very beautiful.
.....................................................


Suddenly, it started to rain.
Everybody ran here and there to find shelter.
All the lanterns got soaked in the rain.
Soon their lights went out
leaving the whole jungle in darkness.
Not a single lantern was saved in the rain.
.....................................................


All of a sudden, one big lantern started
to blink its lights on and off in the darkness.
It almost looked as if a piece of the starry sky had
fallen down.
"Whose lantern is that?"
Everybody wanted to know.
"It is my lantern", little baby rabbit replied proudly.
.....................................................

"Well, well. Look at that! It is the biggest lantern.
It has not broken even in the rain
and it is still lighted.
How did you make it baby rabbit?"
the king wanted to know.
"My friend lightbug helped me. He has asked
thousands of his lightbug friends to come and sit
on the biggest tree in the jungle, with their lights
switching on and off", said the baby rabbit.
"And our lights don't get turned off even in the
rain", explained the lightbugs.
.....................................................


How very thoughtful !
We must help each other, when we live together.
Unity is the strength.
Therefore, baby rabbit and his lightbug friends are
the winners of this competition", declared the king.
Everybody agreed, and cheered.
It was a happy Wesak for everyone in the jungle.
